GLP-1 evidence review

Every study the pipeline pulled on GLP-1 drugs prescribed in the US, rated for source bias and study quality on a 0 to 1 scale, where higher is better on both. Each study shows the inputs behind its scores. Saved decisions sync to data/glp1_studies_draft.json when Claude pulls them.

These are draft ratings written by an LLM that could see drug names, so they have not been masked. Mark each study approved, edited or rejected before it feeds the claim matrix.
